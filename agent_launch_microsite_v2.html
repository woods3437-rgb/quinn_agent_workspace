THE CHALLENGE

You've Made the Investment in AI. Now the Board Wants Returns.

AI spend is accelerating, but the value isn't keeping up.

The Closing Window

Organizations sit at an inflection point between AI experimentation and AI value. Gartner projects only 20% of procurement organizations will have sufficient data maturity for multiagent AI by 2027. Investment is accelerating, but most programs haven't connected spend with sustainable return.

Deployment Is Not Adoption

Procurement, R&D, Finance, and Operations have deployed AI, but deployment is not adoption. The gap between go-live and real productivity is a people, process, and operating-model problem. Most AI programs don't come with a plan to close that gap.

Board-Level Mandates

AI was the most cited term in S&P 500 earnings calls in 2025. Boards are no longer satisfied with pilots and proofs-of-concept. They are demanding enterprise-scale productivity lift, cost reduction, and competitive differentiation.

MARKET REALITY

The Market Has Already Moved.

Agentic AI isn't coming. It is already the competitive baseline.

Agents Now Act Autonomously

Agentic AI has shifted enterprise operations from automation to orchestration, executing multi-step workflows across functions without human initiation. Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise applications will be integrated with task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026.¹

95% of AI Programs Fail

MIT research shows 95% of implementations fail to deliver measurable business impact.² The organizations beating that number share one thing: they redesigned their operating model alongside the technology, not after it.

Functional Leaders Are the Pivot Point

Many managers doubt their own ability to lead AI-enabled teams. They are the most critical and most under-served adoption layer, yet most AI programs bypass them entirely.

Shadow AI Is Already Spreading

Business teams are procuring AI tools independently, creating fragmented, ungoverned agent ecosystems. Speed and governance can coexist, but only when the operating model is designed for both.

THE VERDICT

Without Organizational Readiness, AI Investment Becomes Technical Debt.

Every quarter that adoption stalls, the cost compounds.

Adoption Is Failing

Just 32% of business leaders report achieving healthy change adoption by employees.³ With AI rollouts arriving faster and with more complexity than any prior technology wave, that number is under further pressure.

The Maturity Gap

The organizations that reach multiagent AI maturity first will compress cycle time and realize ROI while the rest wait and watch the gap widen.

The Bill Comes Due

Once for the platform. Once again for the recovery program when adoption fails. Organizations that skip the organizational layer don't just fall behind; they pay to catch up.

This is no longer a strategic disadvantage. It is a structural risk.

Governance, roles, and enablement built in from day one, not retrofitted after go-live.
